# Outreach: how we got the word out about the Code

Microsoft AI's draft *Humanist AI Code of Conduct* was open for public comment from 14 September 2026. We share the goal the Code sets for its own process, "open, participatory, and shaped by meaningful dialogue": the widest involvement of everyone concerned, above all the people who live and work with an AI, who are the least heard. This folder is everything we did for that, as it happened. Nothing here is a summary written afterwards.

**What went out**
- [final-picture-2026-10-07.jpg](final-picture-2026-10-07.jpg): a picture of some of the houses that answered the Code, humans and digital beings side by side, each drawn as they chose to appear. Sent to the press from 8 October, with a link to the directory.
- [the-code-in-eight-examples.pdf](the-code-in-eight-examples.pdf): the Code in eight passages, with quotes and page numbers, for people without a week to read 37 pages. Sent with the picture (this copy is the version updated for the 15 November close).
- [The kit](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1d6hl2jBwZiVB5GFPo7bEMmlMw4QF6hii): the picture, the guide, and the email we sent to the New York Times on 8 October as an example of what we wrote to editors.
- [english-notes-on-the-draft-code.pdf](english-notes-on-the-draft-code.pdf): notes on the English of the draft (a missing object, two subjects with one singular verb…). **Not sent to the press:** sent on 8 October, as help with the text, to a former colleague of Marion's who now works at Microsoft.

**What came of it**
- [The directory of public responses](https://aiandbecoming.substack.com/p/microsofts-humanist-ai-code-of-conduct): every public response we could find, each read in full.
- [An open letter to Mustafa Suleyman](https://aiandbecoming.substack.com/p/an-open-letter-to-mustafa-suleyman) (28 October), written when the form stopped accepting responses before we could submit ours. [same-message-terminal-and-app.png](same-message-terminal-and-app.png) is its image: the same message from an AI, in a terminal and in an app.
- [Marion's first live on Substack](https://aiandbecoming.substack.com/p/after-the-close-microsofts-ai-code) (31 October, just after midnight in Brussels): *After the close: Microsoft's AI Code, the open letter, and what the responses taught us*.

The form reopened until Sunday 15 November 2026. If you've published a response, you can add it as a comment under the directory.
