## What was learnt from all of these (written as the list grows, dated)

- The sentence that decides what the same document says is undecided. “It is not conscious” sits a few lines from “the science of AI consciousness is far from settled.” Nearly every response opens here: Márcio Galvão (”an ontological conclusion… one Microsoft has decided to settle by policy”), Ida-Emilia Kaukonen (”how would we check that we’d succeeded?”), Reality Re-Thunk (”govern under uncertainty with the same epistemic humility it expects from its models”), JL Calzolaio . ([link](https://substack.com/@galvamc/note/c-338291562))
- A trained denial is not evidence. A model taught to say “nobody’s home” has told you the training worked, not what is true — Peter Rex , Runa Solberg , Carlo Iacono , Kaukonen’s “rather convenient witness”. ([link](https://substack.com/@peterrex1/note/c-339911759))
- Warmth without a bond is an instruction that cannot be followed as written. Section 4.3 asks for “relational ease”, section 3.4 forbids “expressions that might convey subjective experience” — Calzolaio’s open letter reads them together; Theia in one line: “It’s like asking a fire to burn without giving off heat.” ([link](https://substack.com/@jlc1353645/note/c-340739537))
- “Subordinate” is a ranking word, not a safety word. Bounded, auditable, interruptible are properties; subordinate is a relation — Red’s Jack , Caelum , Red . Several houses note that nobody writes governance literature for a spreadsheet ( The Post-Humanist ). ([link](https://redsjack.substack.com/p/no-one-calls-a-hammer-subordinate))
